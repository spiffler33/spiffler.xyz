house-md

The medicine behind the mysteries — explained like you're brand new to it. One episode at a time, no medical knowledge needed.

S01E01 · "Pilot"
The Teacher Who Couldn't Talk
A healthy young teacher collapses, has a seizure, and suddenly can't speak — she just babbles. Every doctor says brain tumor. House says everybody lies. What's really hiding in her head?
S01E02 · "Paternity"
The Lacrosse Star with Night Terrors
A fit 16-year-old starts seeing double, wakes screaming from night terrors, and twitches while wide awake. Two brain doctors give up. House finds the answer in the last place anyone looks — the family tree.
S01E03 · "Occam's Razor"
The Young Man Whose Symptoms Didn't Add Up
A healthy young man collapses, and the symptoms keep piling up — a cough, a rash, fever, then his heart and kidneys start to fail. No single disease fits all of it. House's rule: the simplest answer wins. So what could possibly be simpler than a disease?
S01E04 · "Maternity"
The Newborns Who All Got Sick at Once
In the hospital nursery, healthy newborns start crashing one after another — fevers, seizures, failing hearts. Every doctor sees a coincidence. House sees an epidemic. But what could strike a dozen babies at once and leave every grown-up around them perfectly fine?
S01E05 · "Damned If You Do"
The Nun Whose Heart Stopped Over a Rash
A nun comes in with a simple rash on her hands. The doctor gives her an allergy pill — and minutes later her heart stops. Everyone blames the doctor. But seal her in a room with nothing to be allergic to and she still reacts. So what is she allergic to, if it's nowhere in the room?
S01E06 · "The Socratic Method"
The Woman Everyone Called Crazy
For years, doctors called Lucy schizophrenic and stopped looking. Then she turns up with a blood clot she's twenty years too young to have — and she's bleeding and clotting at the very same time. What if the thing wrong with her body and the thing "wrong with her mind" are the same hidden thing?
S01E07 · "Fidelity"
The Woman Who Couldn't Stay Awake
A young woman starts sleeping eighteen hours a day, burning with fever, slipping toward a coma — and every test comes back clean. House recognizes the culprit almost at once: a parasite that only lives in Africa. One problem — she's never left the country. So how did it get inside her?
S01E08 · "Poison"
The Boys Whose Hearts Were Slowing Down
A healthy teenager collapses mid-exam with a heart slowing to a crawl, then starts to seize — and every test comes back clean. Minutes later a second teenager arrives with the exact same symptoms, a kid he's never met. House is sure it's poison. But what strikes two strangers on the same morning, and where is it hiding?
