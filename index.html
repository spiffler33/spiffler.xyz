games

station-nova
a solar flare locked the station down and the crew left. wake her up room by room with PIP, a maintenance robot who has lost most of his memory. no fail states, no timers counting down.
star-shooters
a penalty shootout that is a mind game, not a reflex test. pick your taker, read the keeper, then take a turn in goal.
gravity-golf
space mini-golf where the hazards are gravity wells. slingshot past the planets, sink the black hole.
fluid

word-runner

physics-playground
